# Courtade's volume conjecture for Minkowski sums is false

**Status:** refuted
**Certificate level:** exact

## Statement

The closing question of Courtade's ISIT 2017 paper "Concavity of entropy
power: equivalent formulations and generalizations" asks whether, for bounded
convex sets K, L, B in R^d,

    |K+L+B|^(1/d) |B|^(1/d) + |K|^(1/d) |L|^(1/d)  <=  |K+B|^(1/d) |L+B|^(1/d),

with |.| the d-dimensional volume and + the Minkowski sum — the geometric
analogue of the entropy-power inequality proved in that paper. Unlike the rest
of this archive the refutation is hand-made, and several independent
counterexamples (Fradelizi–Madiman–Zvavitch via sharp Plünnecke–Ruzsa
constants; Skorupinski's zonotope) are in the literature; hence the appendix.

## Counterexample

A zonotope B in R^4 with seven integer generators and two integer segments K
and L with volumes |B| = 28, |K+B| = 68, |L+B| = 44, |K+L+B| = 108. Since
segments have volume zero, the conjectured inequality asserts, in fourth
powers, 28·108 = 3024 <= 2992 = 68·44 — false. Thickening the segments into
boxes of side 1/100 keeps the violation with all three sets full-dimensional,
and padding B with a unit cube extends it to every d >= 4. Every volume is a
sum of |det| over generator quadruples, so the whole certificate is integer
respectively rational arithmetic.

## How to verify

```sh
python verify.py
```

`verify.py` raises an `AssertionError` if any exact check fails; on success it
prints a `PASS` line and (re)generates the artifacts below. When SageMath is
on the PATH, `sage verify_courtade.sage` independently recomputes every volume
by building each Minkowski sum as a vertex polytope over Q and triangulating.

## Artifacts

- `artifacts/certificate.json` — integer generators of the zonotope and the
  two segments, the four integer volumes, the violated products 2992 < 3024,
  and the exact rational volumes of the thickened full-dimensional variant.
