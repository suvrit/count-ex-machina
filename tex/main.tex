\begin{abstract}
  This document reports over 15 counterexamples found using GPT Pro over the course of 12 months, for problems in combinatorics, number theory, convexity, analysis, and other areas. The counterexamples concern both (reasonably) well-known problems as well as lesser known ones. Our counterexamples are accompanied by the repo \href{https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}{\fontfamily{ppl}\selectfont\itshape https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}, and we warmly welcome new contributions toward helping building a rich \emph{``open-source countex library.''}
\end{abstract}
