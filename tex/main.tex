\begin{abstract}
  This document reports 17 counterexamples found using GPT (Pro versions) over the course of the past 12 months, spanning combinatorics, number theory, convexity, functional analysis, among other areas. The counterexamples concern  both (reasonably) well-known problems as well as lesser known ones. Our counterexample collection is accompanied by: \href{https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}{\fontfamily{ppl}\selectfont\itshape https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}, and we warmly welcome new contributions to this repository, hoping to help build an \emph{``open-source database of counterexamples.''}
\end{abstract}
