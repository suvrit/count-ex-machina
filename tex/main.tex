\begin{abstract}
  This document reports 17 counterexamples found using GPT (Pro versions) over the course of the past 12 months, spanning combinatorics, number theory, convextiy, functional analysis, among other areas. The counterexamples concern  both well-known problems as well as lesser known ones. The rapid proliferation of AI assisted counterexamples in the literature at the moment could be attributed to the inherent asymmetry between proving a claim versus finding a single witness that refutes a claim. Our counterexample collection is supported by the repository: \href{https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}{\fontfamily{ppl}\selectfont\itshape https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}, and we warmly welcome contributions from the wider community, hoping to help build an \emph{``open-source database of counterexamples.''}
\end{abstract}
