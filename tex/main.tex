\begin{abstract}
  This document reports GPT driven counterexamples to a number of conjectures, including well-known published conjectures as well as plausible ones made by the author over the course of a decade. Given the asymmetry between proving (typically a \emph{for all} statement) and finding a counterexample (typically a \emph{there exists}), it is no surprise that both the literature and our list of counterexamples are growing rapidly. Code for constructing and verifying the counterexamples is available at: \href{https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}{\fontfamily{ppl}\selectfont\itshape https://github.com/suvrit/count-ex-machina}.
\end{abstract}
