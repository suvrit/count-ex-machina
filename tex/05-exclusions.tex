\section{Excluded and withdrawn items}

The exclusion ledger is part of the mathematical result: it prevents the catalogue from converting route failures into conjecture disproofs.

\begin{audit}[\statusproved: the core stable Schur-positivity problem is not resolved here]
The polynomial in Theorems~\ref{thm:pot}--\ref{thm:common-A} is Schur-positive.  Two previously proposed negative-Schur-coefficient cubics and quartics were rechecked and found not to be stable.  They are withdrawn.  AIM Problems 36 and 37 are therefore not listed as disproved.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[\statusproved: Foulkes is not among the archive disproofs]
The identity $h_2[h_3]-h_3[h_2]=-s_{(2,2,2)}$ concerns the reversed parameter order and does not contradict the Foulkes conjecture in its stated range.  It is excluded.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[\statusproved: the log-volume distance claim is withheld]
An earlier draft advertised a planar-zonotope triangle-inequality violation for the square root of
\[
\log\Vol((K+L)/2)-\tfrac12\log\Vol K-\tfrac12\log\Vol L.
\]
Regenerating the search from the distributed code produced no violation; the claimed certificate data were absent.  The dossier is withdrawn pending a genuine witness.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[\statusproved: route-killers are not catalogue entries]
Excluded examples include failures of a DPP cubic sufficient condition, pointwise Gaussian comparison surrogates, resolvent monotonicity, noncommutative word straightening, Fej\'er truncation barriers, and proposed Ouna\"ies lifts.  Each arose as an intermediate exploration rather than as the statement under investigation.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[\statusproved: unrecovered witnesses are withheld]
The archive index records a direct counterexample to the unshifted matrix inverse inclusion--exclusion problem at $n=6$ and a trace counterexample at $n=10$, but the exact matrices were not recoverable from the currently accessible transcript store.  Neither claim is included without its witness.
\end{audit}
