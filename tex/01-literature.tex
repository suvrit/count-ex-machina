\section{AI-assisted counterexamples}
rapid proliferation of AI assisted counterexamples in the literature at the moment could be attributed to the inherent asymmetry between proving a claim versus finding a single witness that refutes a claim. AI driven progress in mathematics is on a steep rise. A striking concentration of high-value counterexamples appeared in 2026---Table~\ref{tab:literature} recaps some of these. The empirical premise : generative models have repeatedly located explicit witnesses to statements that had resisted conventional attack.  Each row points to a public mathematical source.
