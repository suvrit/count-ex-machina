\section{AI-assisted counterexamples in the literature}

A striking concentration of high-value counterexamples appeared in 2026.  Table~\ref{tab:literature} is placed first because it is the empirical premise of this paper: generative models have repeatedly located explicit witnesses to statements that had resisted conventional attack.  Each row points to a public mathematical source, not merely to a product announcement.

The examples differ sharply in proof technology, but their certificate profiles are simple: a finite object is proposed, the defining hypotheses are checked, and one strict inequality or collision is verified.  That asymmetry is the operative advantage of counterexample search.
